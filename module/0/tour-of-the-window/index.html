A tour of the Power BI Desktop window

In about 10 minutes you will be able to name the ribbon, the canvas, the three view icons, and the two right-side panes without hesitating.
Dalam sekitar 10 menit kamu bakal bisa menyebut ribbon, canvas, tiga ikon view, dan dua panel kanan tanpa ragu.

⏱ 10 min 📊 Difficulty: Easy ✅ You need first: Power BI Desktop installed and open.
No download needed. This is a reading lesson — just follow along. Nggak perlu download apa-apa, cukup dibaca.

The window looks busy at first. It isn't. There are only five areas that matter, and once you can name them, nothing here surprises you again.

Jendelanya kelihatan ramai di awal. Sebenarnya nggak kok. Cuma ada lima area yang penting, dan begitu kamu bisa menyebut namanya, nggak ada lagi yang bikin kaget.

  1. Find the ribbon across the top. It holds every command, grouped into tabs like Home, Insert, and View.

    Temukan ribbon di bagian paling atas. Di sini semua perintah, dikelompokkan jadi tab seperti Home, Insert, dan View.

  2. Look at the big white middle. This is the canvas. Right now it is empty because you have no data yet.

    Lihat area putih besar di tengah. Ini canvas. Sekarang kosong karena kamu belum punya data.

  3. On the far left edge, find three small icons stacked vertically. These switch between Report view, Table view, and Model view. You will meet each one later.

    Di tepi paling kiri, temukan tiga ikon kecil bertumpuk vertikal. Ini untuk berpindah antara Report view, Table view, dan Model view. Nanti kamu kenalan sama masing-masing.

  4. On the right, find two panes: Visualizations (the chart types you can add) and Fields (the columns from your data). They are empty now — that is normal.

    Di kanan, temukan dua panel: Visualizations (jenis grafik yang bisa ditambah) dan Fields (kolom dari datamu). Sekarang kosong — itu wajar.

✓ Checkpoint — you should now see this

Point at your screen and name all five areas out loud: ribbon, canvas, view icons, Visualizations, Fields. If you can do that, this module is done.

Tunjuk layarmu dan sebutkan kelima area itu keras-keras: ribbon, canvas, ikon view, Visualizations, Fields. Kalau kamu bisa, modul ini selesai.

Why this matters / Kenapa ini penting

Every tutorial you ever read will say things like "in the Fields pane, drag…". If those words already mean something to you, you follow instructions three times faster.

Setiap tutorial yang kamu baca nanti bakal bilang hal kayak "di panel Fields, seret…". Kalau kata-kata itu sudah punya makna buat kamu, kamu mengikuti instruksi tiga kali lebih cepat.

Common mistakes / Kesalahan yang sering terjadi

  • Panicking that the panes are empty / Panik karena panelnya kosong
    Empty Visualizations and Fields panes are correct before you load data. They fill up in Module 1. Panel Visualizations dan Fields yang kosong itu benar sebelum kamu memuat data. Nanti terisi di Modul 1.
  • Clicking every ribbon button to see what happens / Mengklik semua tombol ribbon buat lihat efeknya
    Tempting, but you will get lost. Follow the lessons and each button gets introduced when you need it. Menggoda, tapi kamu bakal tersesat. Ikuti pelajaran dan setiap tombol diperkenalkan saat kamu memang butuh.
