Split, replace, and close & apply

In about 12 minutes you will be able to replace a bad value, split a column, and load your cleaned data back into Power BI.
Dalam sekitar 12 menit kamu bakal bisa mengganti nilai yang salah, memecah kolom, dan memuat data bersihmu kembali ke Power BI.

⏱ 12 min 📊 Difficulty: Medium ✅ You need first: Types fixed from the last lesson.
Practice dataset: Messy orders Finish cleaning the same file. / Selesaikan pembersihan file yang sama.
⬇ Download dataset

Two more everyday moves — replacing typos and splitting a crammed column — then we lock in all your work with Close & Apply.

Dua gerakan sehari-hari lagi — mengganti typo dan memecah kolom yang terlalu padat — lalu kita kunci semua kerjaanmu dengan Close & Apply.

  1. The City column has "Jakata" misspelled. Right-click that column and choose Replace Values.

    Kolom City punya "Jakata" yang salah ketik. Klik kanan kolom itu dan pilih Replace Values.

    Menu path: Right-click column ▸ Replace Values

  2. Type Jakata in "Value To Find" and Jakarta in "Replace With", then click OK.

    Ketik Jakata di "Value To Find" dan Jakarta di "Replace With", lalu klik OK.

  3. The Product column holds "Coffee - Latte" style values. Select it, then Home ▸ Split Column ▸ By Delimiter, choose the dash, and click OK.

    Kolom Product berisi nilai gaya "Coffee - Latte". Pilih kolomnya, lalu Home ▸ Split Column ▸ By Delimiter, pilih tanda strip, dan klik OK.

    Menu path: Home ▸ Split Column ▸ By Delimiter

  4. Happy with the data? On the Home tab click Close & Apply. The editor closes and your clean data loads into Power BI.

    Sudah puas dengan datanya? Di tab Home klik Close & Apply. Editor menutup dan data bersihmu masuk ke Power BI.

    Menu path: Home ▸ Close & Apply

✓ Checkpoint — you should now see this

You are back in the main Power BI window, the messy table now sits clean in the Fields pane, and a short "applying changes" bar flashed by.

Kamu kembali ke jendela utama Power BI, tabel berantakan tadi sekarang bersih di panel Fields, dan bar "applying changes" singkat sempat lewat.

Why this matters / Kenapa ini penting

Replace and Split handle the two messiest things in real data: typos and squashed-together values. Close & Apply is the handshake that carries your recipe into the rest of Power BI.

Replace dan Split menangani dua hal paling berantakan di data asli: typo dan nilai yang menempel jadi satu. Close & Apply adalah jabat tangan yang membawa resepmu ke seluruh Power BI.

Common mistakes / Kesalahan yang sering terjadi

  • Forgetting to click Close & Apply / Lupa mengklik Close & Apply
    Your cleaning stays trapped in the editor until you apply it. No apply, no clean data in your charts. Pembersihanmu terjebak di editor sampai kamu menerapkannya. Nggak apply, nggak ada data bersih di grafik.
  • Splitting on the wrong character / Memecah pakai karakter yang salah
    Match the delimiter to what's actually between your values — a dash, a space, or a comma. Preview before OK. Cocokkan delimiter dengan yang benar-benar ada di antara nilaimu — strip, spasi, atau koma. Lihat pratinjau sebelum OK.
