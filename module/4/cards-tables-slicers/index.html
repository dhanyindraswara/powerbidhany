Cards, tables, and slicers

In about 13 minutes you will be able to add a big-number card and a slicer that filters the whole page.
Dalam sekitar 13 menit kamu bakal bisa menambah card angka besar dan slicer yang memfilter seluruh halaman.

⏱ 13 min 📊 Difficulty: Easy ✅ You need first: Your first chart built.
Practice dataset: Sales and products Keep working in the same file. / Lanjut kerja di file yang sama.
⬇ Download dataset

A chart alone isn't a dashboard. Add a card for the headline number and a slicer so viewers can filter.

Grafik saja belum dashboard. Tambah card untuk angka utama dan slicer supaya penonton bisa memfilter.

  1. Click an empty part of the canvas so no chart is selected. In Visualizations, click the Card icon.

    Klik bagian kosong canvas supaya nggak ada grafik terpilih. Di Visualizations, klik ikon Card.

    Menu path: Visualizations pane ▸ Card

  2. Tick Amount. The card shows one big total sales number.

    Centang Amount. Card menampilkan satu angka total penjualan yang besar.

  3. Click empty canvas again, then click the Slicer icon in Visualizations.

    Klik canvas kosong lagi, lalu klik ikon Slicer di Visualizations.

    Menu path: Visualizations pane ▸ Slicer

  4. Tick Product Name. The slicer fills with tick-boxes for each product.

    Centang Product Name. Slicer terisi kotak centang untuk tiap produk.

  5. Tick a product in the slicer. Watch the card and the chart both update to just that product. One slicer filters the whole page.

    Centang satu produk di slicer. Lihat card dan grafik keduanya berubah jadi cuma produk itu. Satu slicer memfilter seluruh halaman.

✓ Checkpoint — you should now see this

Ticking a product in the slicer instantly changes both the card number and the bar chart. Untick to see everything again.

Mencentang satu produk di slicer langsung mengubah angka card dan bar chart. Batalkan centang untuk melihat semuanya lagi.

Why this matters / Kenapa ini penting

Cards give the headline, slicers give control. Together they're what makes a page feel like a real, clickable dashboard instead of a static picture.

Card memberi judul angka, slicer memberi kendali. Bersama-sama itulah yang bikin halaman terasa seperti dashboard beneran yang bisa diklik, bukan gambar diam.

Common mistakes / Kesalahan yang sering terjadi

  • Building the slicer on top of a chart / Membangun slicer di atas grafik
    Always click empty canvas first. Otherwise you change the selected chart instead of making a new visual. Selalu klik canvas kosong dulu. Kalau nggak, kamu mengubah grafik terpilih alih-alih membuat visual baru.
  • Forgetting a slicer selection is still on / Lupa pilihan slicer masih menyala
    If numbers look too small, check whether a slicer is still filtering. Clear it to see the full picture. Kalau angka kelihatan terlalu kecil, cek apakah slicer masih memfilter. Bersihkan untuk melihat gambaran penuh.
