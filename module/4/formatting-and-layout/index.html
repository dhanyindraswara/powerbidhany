Formatting and layout

In about 12 minutes you will be able to give a visual a clear title and line everything up neatly.
Dalam sekitar 12 menit kamu bakal bisa memberi visual judul yang jelas dan merapikan tata letaknya.

⏱ 12 min 📊 Difficulty: Easy ✅ You need first: A card, slicer, and chart on the page.
Practice dataset: Sales and products Same file, keep going. / File yang sama, lanjut terus.
⬇ Download dataset

A tidy page earns trust. We'll title a chart and align the visuals using the Format pane — the paint-roller icon.

Halaman yang rapi mengundang kepercayaan. Kita bakal memberi judul grafik dan merapikan visual pakai panel Format — ikon roller cat.

  1. Click your bar chart to select it. In the Visualizations pane, click the Format icon (a paint roller).

    Klik bar chart-mu untuk memilihnya. Di panel Visualizations, klik ikon Format (roller cat).

    Menu path: Visualizations pane ▸ Format (paint roller)

  2. Find General ▸ Title. Turn it on if needed, then type a clear title like Sales by product.

    Temukan General ▸ Title. Nyalakan kalau perlu, lalu ketik judul jelas seperti Sales by product.

    Menu path: Format ▸ General ▸ Title

  3. Drag the corners of each visual so the slicer is narrow on the left and the chart fills the space. Line up their top edges.

    Seret sudut tiap visual supaya slicer sempit di kiri dan grafik mengisi ruang. Sejajarkan tepi atasnya.

  4. Give the whole page a title: Insert ▸ Text box, type Coffee sales dashboard, and place it at the top.

    Beri seluruh halaman judul: Insert ▸ Text box, ketik Coffee sales dashboard, dan letakkan di atas.

    Menu path: Insert ▸ Text box

✓ Checkpoint — you should now see this

Your page has a heading at the top, a titled chart, and visuals that line up on a tidy grid instead of overlapping.

Halamanmu punya heading di atas, grafik berjudul, dan visual yang sejajar di grid rapi alih-alih saling menumpuk.

Why this matters / Kenapa ini penting

Nobody trusts a messy dashboard, even if the numbers are right. Ten minutes of tidying is the difference between "looks amateur" and "looks like the person knows what they're doing."

Nggak ada yang percaya dashboard berantakan, walau angkanya benar. Sepuluh menit merapikan adalah beda antara "kelihatan amatir" dan "kelihatan seperti orang yang tahu apa yang dikerjakan."

Common mistakes / Kesalahan yang sering terjadi

  • Over-decorating with colours and borders / Menghias berlebihan dengan warna dan garis
    Restraint reads as professional. A title and clean alignment beat rainbow colours every time. Menahan diri terbaca profesional. Judul dan kerapian mengalahkan warna pelangi setiap saat.
  • Leaving the default "Amount by Product Name" title / Membiarkan judul bawaan "Amount by Product Name"
    The auto title is a placeholder. A plain-English title tells the reader what they're looking at. Judul otomatis itu cuma placeholder. Judul berbahasa manusia memberi tahu pembaca apa yang mereka lihat.
