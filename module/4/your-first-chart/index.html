Your first chart

In about 12 minutes you will be able to build a bar chart showing sales by product.Dalam sekitar 12 menit kamu bakal bisa membuat bar chart yang menampilkan penjualan per produk.

⏱ 12 minmenit DifficultyTingkat: EasyGampang You need firstButuh dulu: Module 3 finished, tables loaded and linked.Modul 3 selesai, tabel dimuat dan terhubung.
Practice datasetDataset latihan: Sales and products The linked tables from Module 3.Tabel terhubung dari Modul 3.
⬇ Download dataset

This is the moment it clicks. In three drags you'll turn your numbers into a bar chart. Switch to Report view (the top icon on the left edge) first.

Ini momen semuanya ngeklik. Dalam tiga tarikan kamu mengubah angkamu jadi bar chart. Pindah ke Report view dulu (ikon paling atas di tepi kiri).

  1. In the Visualizations pane, click the Clustered bar chart icon. An empty chart box appears on the canvas.

    Di panel Visualizations, klik ikon Clustered bar chart. Kotak grafik kosong muncul di canvas.

    Menu path: Visualizations pane ▸ Clustered bar chart

  2. In the Fields pane, tick Product Name from the Products table. It drops into the chart's Y-axis.

    Di panel Fields, centang Product Name dari tabel Products. Dia masuk ke Y-axis grafik.

  3. Now tick Amount from the Sales table. Bars appear instantly, one per product.

    Sekarang centang Amount dari tabel Sales. Batang langsung muncul, satu per produk.

  4. Notice this worked across two tables: names from Products, amounts from Sales. That's your relationship doing its job.

    Perhatikan ini bekerja lintas dua tabel: nama dari Products, jumlah dari Sales. Itu relationship-mu sedang bekerja.

✓ Checkpoint — you should now see thisCheckpoint — sekarang kamu harusnya lihat ini

A horizontal bar chart sits on your canvas with a labelled bar for each product, longest bar for the best seller.

Bar chart horizontal ada di canvas-mu dengan batang berlabel untuk tiap produk, batang terpanjang untuk yang paling laku.

Why this mattersKenapa ini penting

The tick-a-field, get-a-chart loop is how you'll build every visual from now on. Once this feels natural, the whole tool opens up.

Loop centang-field, dapat-grafik adalah cara kamu membangun setiap visual mulai sekarang. Begitu ini terasa alami, seluruh tool-nya terbuka.

Common mistakesKesalahan yang sering terjadi

  • Ticking a field with no chart selectedMencentang field tanpa grafik terpilih
    Power BI makes a brand-new visual instead of filling yours. Click your chart first, then tick fields.Power BI membuat visual baru alih-alih mengisi punyamu. Klik grafikmu dulu, baru centang field.
  • Putting a text field where a number belongsMenaruh field teks di tempat angka
    Amount must be a number (Module 2). If bars don't appear, check that its type isn't stuck as text.Amount harus angka (Modul 2). Kalau batang nggak muncul, cek tipenya jangan sampai nyangkut jadi teks.
