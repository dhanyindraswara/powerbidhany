Your first measure

In about 13 minutes you will be able to write a Total Sales measure using SUM and show it on a card.
Dalam sekitar 13 menit kamu bakal bisa menulis measure Total Sales pakai SUM dan menampilkannya di card.

⏱ 13 min 📊 Difficulty: Medium ✅ You need first: Sales table loaded.
Practice dataset: Sales and products The linked tables you already know. / Tabel terhubung yang sudah kamu kenal.
⬇ Download dataset

Time to write actual DAX — Power BI's formula language. Your first one is three words long. Don't be scared.

Saatnya menulis DAX beneran — bahasa formula Power BI. Yang pertama cuma tiga kata. Jangan takut.

  1. In the Fields pane, right-click the Sales table and choose New measure.

    Di panel Fields, klik kanan tabel Sales dan pilih New measure.

    Menu path: Right-click Sales table ▸ New measure

  2. A formula bar appears at the top. Type exactly: Total Sales = SUM(Sales[Amount])

    Formula bar muncul di atas. Ketik persis: Total Sales = SUM(Sales[Amount])

  3. As you type Sales[, a suggestion list pops up. Pick Amount from it rather than typing the whole thing. Press Enter to finish.

    Saat kamu mengetik Sales[, daftar saran muncul. Pilih Amount dari situ daripada mengetik semuanya. Tekan Enter untuk menyelesaikan.

  4. Total Sales now appears in the Fields pane with a little calculator icon. Add a Card visual and tick Total Sales to see it.

    Total Sales sekarang muncul di panel Fields dengan ikon kalkulator kecil. Tambah visual Card dan centang Total Sales untuk melihatnya.

✓ Checkpoint — you should now see this

Total Sales sits in the Fields pane with a calculator icon, and a card shows the summed total. Add a product slicer and the total reacts.

Total Sales ada di panel Fields dengan ikon kalkulator, dan card menampilkan total yang dijumlah. Tambah slicer produk dan totalnya bereaksi.

Why this matters / Kenapa ini penting

SUM is the measure you'll write more than any other. Once you've written one measure, every other DAX formula is just a variation on the same shape.

SUM adalah measure yang paling sering kamu tulis. Begitu kamu menulis satu measure, tiap formula DAX lain cuma variasi dari bentuk yang sama.

Common mistakes / Kesalahan yang sering terjadi

  • Typing the column name instead of picking it / Mengetik nama kolom alih-alih memilihnya
    One typo and the measure errors. Always choose from the suggestion list so the name is exact. Satu typo dan measure-nya error. Selalu pilih dari daftar saran supaya namanya persis.
  • Confusing New measure with New column / Bingung antara New measure dan New column
    They sit next to each other in the menu. For totals you want New measure, every time. Keduanya bersebelahan di menu. Untuk total kamu mau New measure, tiap saat.
