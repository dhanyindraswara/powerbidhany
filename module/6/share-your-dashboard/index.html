Share your dashboard

In about 10 minutes you will be able to send a colleague a link to your report and understand who can open it.
Dalam sekitar 10 menit kamu bakal bisa mengirim rekan kerja link ke report-mu dan paham siapa yang bisa membukanya.

⏱ 10 min 📊 Difficulty: Easy ✅ You need first: A published report in the Service.
No download needed. This is a reading lesson — just follow along. Nggak perlu download apa-apa, cukup dibaca.

Publishing put your report online. Sharing decides who's allowed to see it. We'll keep it simple and safe.

Publikasi menaruh report-mu online. Sharing menentukan siapa yang boleh melihatnya. Kita bikin sederhana dan aman.

  1. In the browser at app.powerbi.com, open your report. Click the Share button near the top right.

    Di browser di app.powerbi.com, buka report-mu. Klik tombol Share di dekat kanan atas.

    Menu path: app.powerbi.com ▸ open report ▸ Share

  2. Type a colleague's work email. Leave the default permission as view-only unless you truly want them to edit.

    Ketik email kantor rekanmu. Biarkan izin bawaan sebagai view-only kecuali kamu benar-benar ingin mereka mengedit.

  3. Click Send. They get an email with a link. Only people you add can open it — it's not public.

    Klik Send. Mereka menerima email berisi link. Hanya orang yang kamu tambahkan yang bisa membukanya — nggak publik.

  4. Remember: sharing shows people the data too. Only share reports whose numbers those people are allowed to see.

    Ingat: sharing juga memperlihatkan datanya. Hanya bagikan report yang angkanya boleh dilihat orang-orang itu.

✓ Checkpoint — you should now see this

A colleague receives a link, opens your report, and sees the same dashboard — read-only. You've shared safely.

Rekan kerja menerima link, membuka report-mu, dan melihat dashboard yang sama — read-only. Kamu sudah berbagi dengan aman.

Why this matters / Kenapa ini penting

Knowing that sharing exposes the underlying data is the one safety rule that keeps beginners out of trouble at work. Share the view, mind the data.

Tahu bahwa sharing membuka data di baliknya adalah satu aturan keamanan yang menjauhkan pemula dari masalah di kantor. Bagikan tampilannya, jaga datanya.

Common mistakes / Kesalahan yang sering terjadi

  • Granting edit access to everyone / Memberi akses edit ke semua orang
    View-only is the safe default. Only give edit to people who genuinely build the report with you. View-only adalah default yang aman. Beri edit cuma ke orang yang benar-benar membangun report bareng kamu.
  • Sharing sensitive numbers by accident / Berbagi angka sensitif nggak sengaja
    Before you hit Send, ask: is this person allowed to see these numbers? If unsure, don't share yet. Sebelum menekan Send, tanya: apakah orang ini boleh melihat angka ini? Kalau ragu, jangan bagikan dulu.
