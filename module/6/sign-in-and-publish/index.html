Sign in and publish

In about 12 minutes you will be able to publish your report to the Power BI Service and open it in a browser.
Dalam sekitar 12 menit kamu bakal bisa mempublikasikan report-mu ke Power BI Service dan membukanya di browser.

⏱ 12 min 📊 Difficulty: Medium ✅ You need first: A finished report and a work or school Microsoft account.
No download needed. This is a reading lesson — just follow along. Nggak perlu download apa-apa, cukup dibaca.

Publishing sends a copy of your report to Microsoft's website so you can open it anywhere and share a link. You need a work or school account — a personal Gmail won't work for publishing.

Publikasi mengirim salinan report-mu ke website Microsoft supaya kamu bisa membukanya di mana saja dan berbagi link. Kamu butuh akun kantor atau sekolah — Gmail pribadi nggak bisa buat publikasi.

  1. First save your file: File ▸ Save, and give it a name like Coffee Dashboard. Publishing needs a saved file.

    Simpan dulu file-mu: File ▸ Save, dan beri nama seperti Coffee Dashboard. Publikasi butuh file yang tersimpan.

    Menu path: File ▸ Save

  2. Top-right of the window, click Sign in and enter your work or school account.

    Di kanan atas jendela, klik Sign in dan masukkan akun kantor atau sekolahmu.

    Menu path: Top-right ▸ Sign in

  3. On the Home tab, click Publish. If asked where, choose My workspace.

    Di tab Home, klik Publish. Kalau ditanya ke mana, pilih My workspace.

    Menu path: Home ▸ Publish ▸ My workspace

  4. When it finishes, a box shows a link like "Open Coffee Dashboard in Power BI". Click it to see your report live in the browser.

    Setelah selesai, kotak menampilkan link seperti "Open Coffee Dashboard in Power BI". Klik untuk melihat report-mu langsung di browser.

✓ Checkpoint — you should now see this

Your report opens in a web browser at app.powerbi.com, looking just like it did on the Desktop.

Report-mu terbuka di browser web di app.powerbi.com, tampil persis seperti di Desktop.

Why this matters / Kenapa ini penting

A report on your laptop helps nobody but you. Publishing is the step that turns your work into something a team can actually use. It's the whole point of the last five modules.

Report di laptop cuma membantu kamu sendiri. Publikasi adalah langkah yang mengubah kerjaanmu jadi sesuatu yang bisa dipakai tim. Itulah inti dari lima modul terakhir.

Common mistakes / Kesalahan yang sering terjadi

  • Trying to publish with a personal account / Mencoba publikasi dengan akun pribadi
    Publishing needs a work or school account. If you don't have one, you can still do everything up to this step. Publikasi butuh akun kantor atau sekolah. Kalau kamu nggak punya, kamu tetap bisa melakukan semuanya sampai langkah ini.
  • Forgetting to save before publishing / Lupa menyimpan sebelum publikasi
    Power BI will nag you to save first. Just do File ▸ Save and try Publish again. Power BI bakal mengingatkan kamu menyimpan dulu. Cukup File ▸ Save dan coba Publish lagi.
